The platform

Everything a paywall for stories actually needs.

DRM web reader

Pages render one fragment at a time as canvas — never a downloadable file — so a whole book can't walk out the door.

Dynamic watermarking

Every reader's phone number and email is woven faintly across the text — a screenshot points straight back to its source.

Author Studio

A focused editor for drafting, pricing unfinished works, and sending review invitations — Bengali script renders the way it should.

Multi-tenant storefronts

Each publishing house gets an isolated, white-labelled shop — own brand, own domain, own analytics, shared infrastructure.

Scouting analytics

Real-time sales and read-through data turn guesswork into a marketplace for print rights — back the books readers already chose.

AI that reads Bangla

Recommendations from reading history, in-editor grammar help, and pre-screening of uploads — tuned for the language, not bolted on.
